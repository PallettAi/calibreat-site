calibrEAT Website Redesign Concepts

Three exploratory directions for a total visual overhaul

1. The Instrument (Existing Demo)

Instrumentation

The site is treated as a measuring instrument: green for data, brass for calibrated moments, hairlines and corner brackets, monospaced micro‑labels. Light/dark toggle shares token set.

2. Calm & Clear

Minimalist

A serene, spacious layout with a muted palette (soft greys, sage accents). Focus on legibility and hierarchy; generous whitespace, subtle shadows, and rounded containers. CTA uses a single brand colour.

3. Bold & Playful

Vibrant

Energetic and expressive: bold gradients, oversized typography, playful illustrations. Uses a broader colour palette (emerald, brass, vivid cyan) while keeping the core message clear. Interactive elements have micro‑animations.
