Lockup — mark + wordmark

The full logo wherever there's room: website hero & footer, README, about screen. Wordmark styling matches the in-app calibrEAT treatment (calibr in ink, EAT in green). File: calibreat-lockup.svg.

Minimum lockup width ≈ 130 px (below that, use the mark alone). Wordmark is live text here — happy to convert it to outlines for PNG/print export.
03

Usage notes

  • Light surface → calibreat-mark-line.svg (green C, ink beam).
  • Dark surface → calibreat-mark-line-dark.svg (lime beam/C on ink backgrounds).
  • Lockup for headers/footers; standalone mark for buttons, avatars, notifications.
  • Keep a clear margin around the mark equal to ~½ the C-ring height; never stretch, tilt, or recolor the art.
  • Apple = food in · Flame = energy out — the balance is the brand story, keep both present at sizes ≥ 24 px.
  • The tile concepts (calibreat-mark-reference.svg, calibreat-mark-inverted.svg) are archived here if you later want an app-icon tile.
