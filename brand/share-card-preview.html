Static snapshot — not the source of truth. This page mirrors the module by hand, so it can lag behind it. The app's own /share-lab (dev-only) renders the real components, reports any template × format pair that overflows its frame, and is what to design against.

Sample data: a 12-day logging streak, 6/7 days logged, True Burn measured at 2,210 kcal against a 2,310 formula estimate. In the app every number is the user's own, computed on-device — the image is captured locally and shared as a PNG, and nothing about the week is uploaded. The QR is generated from the real module matrix (25 × 25, level M), so it scans straight to the site even from a re-shared screenshot.
