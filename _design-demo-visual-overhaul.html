01 — The rules

Four decisions everything else obeys

Not a re-skin. One idea — a calorie tracker is a measuring device, so the site is dressed like calibrated equipment: quiet, precise, and confident enough to leave things empty.

  • 01Green is the product, brass is the payoff. Today lime is spent on accents, borders, bars, dots and column washes all at once. New rule: green means your data and the app; brass — a single warm — means the calibrated moment, and appears about six times per page, never more.
  • 02Mono micro-labels replace gradient dashes. Every section opens with an uppercase, tracked IBM Plex Mono label and a 7px square instead of the gradient rule. It costs one request and it is the single biggest shift in perceived quality.
  • 03Hairlines over boxes. The twelve feature cards lose their border, fill, radius and shadow. They become a three-column ledger — a hairline top rule, a mono index, a small square icon, and air — with a green underline that draws in on hover.
  • 04The phone sits in a reticle. Corner brackets in brass, a fine 28px grid, a soft focus mask. It reads as a device being measured, which is the whole thesis of the product, and it gives the hero a signature that is not a stock floating phone.

Grid

56px, and 28px inside the reticle, against the current 46px hero plus a six-stop radial wash. Fewer gradients, more alignment.

Brass

“calibrated” and the £191.88 → £4.99 line. The word in the headline and the number in the argument are the same accent, so the sentence and the maths are visually linked.

Radius

22px → 4px. Pills and cards go hard-cornered; circles only where something is genuinely round.

Reticle

Brass corner brackets over a masked grid. Pure CSS, about fourteen lines, no image. It is the one thing here nobody else has.

06 — Rollout

How it lands without touching the funnel

Nine pages. Every page currently inlines the same token block and nav markup, so the cleanest win is a real shared stylesheet first, then the new look dropped on top.

  • 01One stylesheet, nine pages. The token block, nav, footer, buttons and cards are duplicated verbatim across all nine HTML files today. Extract to site.css before restyling, so the redesign is a diff against one file rather than nine.
  • 02Homepage first. It carries the reticle, the feature ledger, the restyled table and the price card — every pattern the other pages reuse.
  • 03Then the two commercial pages. license.html and download.html are where money is decided; they get the brass treatment and a mobile pass on the checkout tap targets.
  • 04Legal last. Privacy, terms and refunds change appearance only — not one word of their copy moves.
  • ◆What I deliberately left alone. The headline, the £191.88 comparison, the unlock-screen-as-hero argument, the compare table's honesty about what calibrEAT does not do, and the VAT-inclusive pricing — all content. And polish.css and site.js keep working untouched, so the reduced-motion and no-JS guarantees stay exactly as they are.
