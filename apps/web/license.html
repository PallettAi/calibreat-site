FAQ

Before you buy

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. £4.99 including VAT, once. A lifetime license unlocks calibrEAT forever — no subscriptions, no tiers, no in-app purchases, and every future update is included.

Does the price include VAT?

Yes. £4.99 is the amount you pay, VAT included. There is no extra charge at checkout for UK VAT.

How do I receive my activation code?

Instantly by email at checkout. It's a permanent code that never expires. Keep it somewhere safe — it's also on your order receipt, and if it ever goes missing you can self-serve a replacement at Resend my code.

Can I use my code on more than one device?

Your code is active on one device at a time — phone / tablet. Use “Deactivate on this device” in the app to free the slot, then activate on the new one. Email support@calibreat.co.uk if you need a reset.

Can I use the app without a license?

No. The welcome screen stays locked until you enter a valid activation code. There is no unpaid or trial mode.

What if I lose my activation email?

Email support@calibreat.co.uk from the address you paid with and we’ll resend your code.

How do I get help?

Email support@calibreat.co.uk. Support is email only.
