Changelog

What’s new in beta v0.0.5

Your data can leave the phone, and come back. Nothing you log is trapped, and nothing you are told is inflated.

Released 21 October 2026. The Android build is finished but not yet published for download.

  • Import your diary from MyFitnessPal or Cronometer

    Settings → “Import from another app” reads the CSV export from either app and adds those days to your diary. It never replaces what you already have, shows a preview first, and importing the same file twice adds nothing.

  • Restore from a backup

    Pick a backup file and see exactly what will change before anything is written. Adding to your data is the default.

  • Your target stops moving when you train

    Logged workouts no longer add to your daily food target. The target comes from your resting burn and your activity level.

  • Yesterday, calibrated

    The first visit to Home after midnight can show a recap of your last full day, once per day. “Not now” dismisses it for that day.

  • Weekly share card

    Trends can turn your week into a 1080×1350 image covering your logging streak, coverage and True Burn drift. It celebrates logging, not weight change, and a rough week is shared as your best logging run.

  • Start with a scan

    Finishing setup opens Home with the barcode scanner ready, so the first thing you log can be a real pack.

  • Clearer instruments

    BMI shows where you sit on a four-band arc, and the water sheet fills up to your goal.

  • UK food data, offline first

    Generic foods come from the bundled CoFID 2021 dataset, so they work without a connection. Packaged products come from Open Food Facts UK, and barcode scans can still check the wider catalogue.

  • Free, with no account

    No subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no ads. Nothing is locked behind a licence, a sign-in or a paywall.
