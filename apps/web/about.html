About PallettAi · Why we exist

Everyday tools should be cheap, honest, and yours.

We’re PallettAi — a new AI development company building software that actually lowers the cost of daily life. calibrEAT is our first proof: a proper calorie and lifestyle tracker without the subscription, without the ads, and without the data grab.

In one sentence: We use AI to build faster and cheaper — then pass that saving to you. One fair price, no tricks, your data stays on your phone. If we can’t explain a decision plainly, we haven’t earned your trust.

We started because the basics got too expensive

Food logging, habit tracking, wellness tools — they all started simple and useful. Then came the £9.99/month, the “premium” paywalls, the banner ads between your meals, and the fine print that says “we may share analytics with partners.”

We’re a small team using modern AI to do what used to take a big studio. That means we can build calibrEAT properly once, charge once, and not need to rent your attention back to advertisers or bundle your health data to stay alive.

Cut costs, not corners

One lifetime activation. No subscription, no “pro” tier. You pay once, your key works forever — including every future update we ship for this app.

No ads. Ever.

We don’t run ads in the app or sell your attention. Your meals aren’t a place for someone else’s billboard.

No data to sell

Your food, water and weight logs live in a local database on your device. We never host them, see them, or sell them. The only thing we store server-side is your activation (email + hashed code) to enforce one device per purchase.

Our highlight · Transparency

Honesty is the feature

Anyone can say “we’re transparent.” Here’s exactly what that means for you — in writing, before you pay.

What you actually pay for The app is free to install. You buy one digital item — a permanent activation code delivered instantly by email. Price is shown before you tick any consent. No hidden renewal.
What “lifetime” means Every update we ship for calibrEAT is included. No v2 paywall for the same app. If we ever build a genuinely new product (different app/brand), that’s a separate choice — we have no such plan.
Why one device per code One active device per email + code keeps sharing honest and supports our low price. Moving phones? Tap “Deactivate on this device” and re-enter the same code — or email us and we’ll free the slot after verifying you own the purchase.
Where your data lives On your phone only (SQLite/AsyncStorage). No calibrEAT account, no cloud sync. Food lookups go directly to Open Food Facts / USDA — not through us. See Privacy.
Refunds without gotchas Delivered instantly = no change-of-mind refund after the code lands (UK law, CCRs reg. 37 — you tick to consent). Faulty, misdescribed or not delivered? We put it right, including a refund where that’s the right remedy. Plain English on Refunds.
How to verify us Checkout is via a Merchant of Record (seller of record, handles VAT/receipt). We link to their terms at checkout. Company details, contact and VAT status will be published in Terms before we take money.

Built with AI, run by people

We’re AI-native, not AI-hype. We use code generation, automated testing and data tooling to build smaller, faster and cheaper — then we stay in the loop to review, test on real devices, and answer your emails.

That’s the impact we want: everyday software that used to cost a subscription now costs a coffee — because intelligence lowered the build cost and we chose to pass it on instead of pocketing it.
