About PallettAi · Who we are

Simple tools, built to be yours.

We’re PallettAi, a small team making everyday software. calibrEAT is our first app: a calorie and lifestyle tracker with no subscription, no ads and no data grab.

See build status Why transparency matters

We use AI to help us build, and we pass the time saved on to you.

Plenty of trackers started out simple, then added monthly fees, paywalls, ads and fine print about selling your data. We’d rather build something simple and leave your attention alone.

Cut costs, not corners

Free to download. No pro tier, no subscription, no renewal.

No ads

Your meals aren’t a billboard. We don’t run ads or sell your attention.

No data to sell

Logs stay on your device. We never host, see or sell them.

Transparency

Plain answers, up front

Here is what you need to know, in plain English.

What it costs Free to download. No account, no activation code, no renewal.
Updates Every release is a plain download. Nothing is locked behind a code.
Where data lives On your phone only. In-app tools (BMI and the like) use that local data and never send it to us. Lookups go direct to Open Food Facts. Read more in our Privacy.
Who we are PallettAi operates calibrEAT. Get in touch any time via the Support page.

We use AI, and people check the work. AI helps us build faster. People review it, test it on real phones and answer your emails at support@calibreat.co.uk or calibreat@proton.me.

calibrEAT is the first of a small set of tools we plan to make. The same rules will apply to each one: free, private, and clear about what it does.
