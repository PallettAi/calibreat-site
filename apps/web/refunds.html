Refunds

Your lifetime code is delivered instantly by email. To keep things fair — a code can’t be “returned” once you’ve seen it — orders are final once the code is delivered. If something hasn’t arrived or isn’t working as it should, we’re here to help and put it right quickly. This page explains how, and your UK statutory rights are always protected.

Last updated: 8 September 2026 · Effective: 8 September 2026 · Contact: support@calibreat.co.uk

Good to know before you buy: At checkout you’ll be asked to tick a box that says “I consent to immediate supply of the activation code and acknowledge I will lose my right to cancel once supply has begun.” As soon as your code is emailed or made available, supply has begun and the 14-day cancellation right for digital content ends (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regs. 29 & 37). You don’t need to have activated the code — delivery itself counts as supply. Think of it like a gift card: once you’ve seen the code it can be used straight away, so we’re unable to offer change-of-mind refunds after delivery. If anything is missing or not working, the remedies below apply and we’ll help you sort it.

How refunds work

We want to keep this simple and fair for everyone:

🚫 Not refundable after delivery

  • Change of mind — even if you haven’t activated the code yet, delivery counts as supply.
  • Device preferences (e.g. prefer Play Store over sideload) where the app works as described on Android 8+ or iOS 15+.
  • Diet results — calorie estimates vary per person; this isn’t a fault.
  • Lost the email? We’ll happily re-send — just ask.

✅ We’ll put it right when

  • Your code was never delivered and we can’t supply it.
  • Something is faulty, misdescribed or incompatible despite following our steps.
  • We missed required checkout information / confirmation.
  • You were charged twice by mistake (duplicate above).

In the right-hand cases we’ll first try the quickest fix that gets you going. If that isn’t possible or would cause significant inconvenience, we’ll arrange a refund — including a full refund where that’s the appropriate remedy.

If something’s faulty or not as described

Your statutory rights are always protected. Here’s what counts and how we resolve it:

  1. What counts as faulty? For example: the code never unlocks on any device that meets our published requirements despite correct entry and our steps, or the unlocked app materially lacks the features described at sale (food diary, water & weight, barcode scan) due to our fault. What doesn’t count: Open Food Facts / device / network behaviour outside our control, or a misunderstanding of what a calorie estimate is (see Not medical advice).
  2. We’ll fix it first — a replacement code, a fix, or alternative access, within a reasonable time and without significant inconvenience to you.
  3. If we can’t fix it promptly, you’re entitled to a price reduction — which can be the full price if the content is essentially not usable for you.
Time limits: The general limitation period for contract claims in England and Wales is 6 years (Limitation Act 1980). We assess each case reasonably on the evidence from both sides — you don’t need to quote legislation to get help.

What happens to your code after a refund

Every refunded or charged-back code is revoked via webhook. The license service stores codes hashed (SHA-256) and marks the binding revoked = true. Afterwards POST /v1/activate and /v1/validate with that code fail and the app re-locks on next online check.

  • We keep the hashed code and the revocation record for fraud prevention — we never keep your card details (the MoR does).
  • Refunded the wrong duplicate by mistake? Email us and we’ll re-issue a fresh code free of charge.
  • Chargebacks: if you ask your bank to reverse the charge, please let us know too — the MoR will notify our license service and the code will be deactivated as above. A chargeback and a refund cover the same order, so they don’t stack. If a code was delivered and used, the MoR may provide delivery and consent evidence when reviewing the dispute.

How to get in touch

We aim to make this painless. Email support@calibreat.co.uk:

  • Subject: Help with my order — [order reference]
  • Your buyer email, order reference / receipt, and date of purchase
  • A short note on what happened — and if it’s technical, your device/OS, app version, and any screenshots or screen recordings. A screenshot of your receipt with the transaction / order ID helps us verify fastest.

We reply within 2 business days and aim to fix or agree next steps within 14 days. If a refund is the right remedy, it goes via the MoR (seller of record) to your original payment method within 14 days of us agreeing it — please allow a few extra days for it to appear. If we don’t think a legal ground applies, we’ll explain why in writing and let you know about ADR (see Terms §17) and your right to go to court. You can also contact the Citizens Advice Consumer Service (03454 04 05 06) — we treat every genuine enquiry with respect.

Refunds are handled in line with UK law for digital content — including the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 and Consumer Rights Act 2015 — with your statutory rights always preserved. Please have a solicitor review the company-details block in Terms §1, the MoR linkage and the checkout tick wording before you start taking payments. We keep a durable copy of the tick record and confirmation for each order.
