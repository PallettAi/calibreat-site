Refunds

Lifetime codes are digital content delivered instantly. Once you’ve consented to immediate delivery and the code is sent, the sale is final — we only refund where UK law requires us to. This page is the whole policy.

Last updated: 8 September 2026 · Effective: 8 September 2026 · Contact: support@calibreat.co.uk

Headline — non-refundable after delivery: When you buy on calibreat.co.uk you tick a box that says “I consent to immediate supply of the activation code and acknowledge I lose my right to cancel once supply has begun.” Once the code is emailed / made available, supply has begun and you do not have a 14-day right to cancel. This is what the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regs. 29 & 37, expressly allow for digital content not on a tangible medium where you give that consent and acknowledgement. Activating the code is not required — delivery itself is supply. We enforce this strictly because a code once seen can be used and cannot be “returned”.

1. The UK law we follow — and nothing more

This is a minimum-refund policy. We give you exactly what UK consumer law requires for digital content not on a tangible medium and no voluntary “cooling-off” beyond it. Two statutes govern it:

LawWhat it says — in plain English
Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 (CCRs) — regs. 13, 16, 29, 36, 37For digital content you buy online you normally have 14 days to cancel. But you lose that right if before buying you (a) consent to immediate supply and (b) acknowledge you will lose the cancellation right once supply begins — and we give you confirmation with that consent on a durable medium before supply (regs. 13, 14, 36). If we have that tick, reg. 37(2) says regs. 37(3)–(8) (refunds on cancellation) do not apply where supply has begun. If we don’t have it, or we never gave you the required pre-contract info / confirmation (regs. 12(5), 16(3)), reg. 37(4) says you do not pay for supply in the cancellation period.
Consumer Rights Act 2015 (CRA) — Chapter 3, ss. 33–47Digital content must be of satisfactory quality, fit for any particular purpose you made known to us, and as described (ss. 34–36). If it isn’t, or we had no right to supply it (s. 33), or it damages your device through lack of reasonable care (s. 46), you have a remedy — but unlike goods, there is no 30-day short-term right to reject for digital content. The remedy is: first repair or replacement; if that’s impossible or not done promptly without significant inconvenience, a price reduction (which can be 100%) (ss. 42–45). If we fail to supply at all, different rules apply. Nothing in our policy limits these statutory rights.

Our checkout implements the CCRs correctly: the tick is mandatory for immediate delivery — without it, we do not deliver within the 14-day window (we wait, or the contract is not concluded). The tick text, the pre-contract information on License and Terms, and the MoR confirmation email (order reference + code + these terms + the acknowledgement copy) together satisfy the CCRs’ information and confirmation requirements.

2. When we do not refund (the default)

● No refund — change of mind after delivery

Once the code has been emailed / made available to you with your consent+acknowledgement tick, the following are not grounds for a refund. This list is not exhaustive — if it isn’t in §3, §4, §5 or §6 below, it is not refundable:

  • Change of mind / “I haven’t used it yet” — delivery is the trigger, not activation. An unused but delivered code is still supplied digital content.
  • “I bought the wrong email” — the code was still delivered to the address you typed at checkout. We will re-issue to the correct verified email where we can, but not refund (see §5).
  • Compatible device complaints that are OS/app-store matters (e.g. you prefer Play Store vs sideload) where the content is as described and works on the stated requirements (Android 8+, iOS 15+, macOS 12+).
  • Diet results — the app estimates calorie needs (Mifflin-St Jeor); individual results vary. This is not a quality failure.
  • Lost or deleted the email — email support@calibreat.co.uk and we will re-send to the buyer address.

4. Duplicate / accidental double purchase

Bought twice in error (e.g. double-clicked, refreshed checkout)? These are refundable — but only the duplicate unused code:

  • Email support@calibreat.co.uk within 14 days with both order references / buyer emails and state which code to keep.
  • We verify neither code from the duplicate order has been activated (if one has, we keep that one live and refund the other). The refunded code is then revoked (see §8).
  • If both codes from the duplicate order are already activated on different devices/emails, we cannot know which was the “mistake” — we treat the second purchase as a genuine second license and the default non-refundable rule applies unless a statutory ground in §3 is made out.

5. Not delivered / you never received the code

The MoR emails the code within seconds. If nothing arrived:

  1. Check spam/junk, the spelling of the buyer email, and the MoR’s customer portal (“My orders” on the receipt). Payment failed = nothing to deliver.
  2. Still nothing 30 minutes after payment succeeded? Email support@calibreat.co.uk with the buyer email + order reference + last 4 of the card / payment method.
  3. We will re-send or re-issue within 2 business days. This is the primary remedy — we supply what you paid for.
  4. If we genuinely cannot supply the content at all (system failure, provider outage), we will refund.

Non-delivery emails where payment never settled with the MoR are not purchases — there is no contract and no code to refund.

6. Faulty, misdescribed or incompatible — the CRA remedy ladder

Nothing in this policy limits your CRA Chapter 3 rights. How it works for a lifetime code:

  1. Digital content not of satisfactory quality, not fit for purpose, or not as described (ss. 34–36) — examples that would count: the code never unlocks on any device meeting the published requirements despite correct entry and our instructions, or the app as unlocked materially lacks the features described at sale (food diary, water & weight, barcode scan) due to our fault. Examples that would not count: Open Food Facts / device / network behaviour outside our control, or a misunderstanding of what a calorie estimate is (see Not medical advice).
  2. First remedy is repair or replacement (s. 43) — we will provide a replacement code, fix the activation, or supply access another way, within a reasonable time and without significant inconvenience to you.
  3. If repair/replacement is impossible, or we fail to do it promptly / without significant inconvenience (s. 44), you are entitled to a price reduction — which can be the full price if the content is essentially worthless to you.
  4. Device damage (s. 46) is separate: if the content damages your device/other content because we failed to use reasonable care & skill, we must repair or compensate — contact us with evidence.
  5. There is no short-term right to reject for digital content (unlike goods). And we are not liable for loss that was not foreseeable or was caused by something outside our reasonable control.
Time limits & burden: The CRA does not set a hard 6-year cut-off for digital remedies — but the general limitation period for contract claims in England and Wales is 6 years (Limitation Act 1980). Within the first 6 months after supply, the evidential practical burden leans heavily on us to show the content was conforming — though for digital content the CRA puts the general burden on the consumer to show the content did not conform. We will act reasonably on evidence from both sides.

7. How to claim a statutory remedy

Email support@calibreat.co.uk with:

  • Subject: Refund / remedy request — [order reference]
  • Buyer email, order reference / MoR receipt, date of purchase
  • What went wrong — which ground in §3 / §6, and the device/OS, app version, and any screenshots or screen recordings showing the failure

We aim to acknowledge within 2 business days and resolve or propose repair/replacement within 14 days.

Refunds, where due, go via the MoR (seller of record) to your original payment method within 14 days of agreement. The MoR — not PallettAi — processes the payment reversal and its timeline may add a few days.

If we do not agree you have a statutory ground, we will say why in writing and tell you about ADR (see Terms §17) and your right to go to court. You may also contact the Citizens Advice Consumer Service (03454 04 05 06) for independent guidance — we will not treat a good-faith claim as a hostile act.

8. What happens to the code after a refund or chargeback

Every refunded or charged-back code is revoked via webhook. The license API stores codes hashed (SHA-256) and marks the binding revoked = true. Thereafter:

  • POST /v1/activate and /v1/validate with that code fail; the app re-locks on next online check.
  • We keep the hashed code and the revocation record for fraud prevention — we do not keep your card details (the MoR does).
  • If a genuine mistake happened (e.g. we refunded the wrong duplicate), email us and we will re-issue a fresh code free of charge.

9. Chargebacks

If you ask your bank/card provider to reverse the charge instead of contacting us, the MoR will notify our license API and the code will be revoked per §8. Chargebacks do not create an extra refund — they are simply another way of obtaining the statutory refund above, and they also trigger revocation. Abusive chargebacks after successful delivery and use may be contested by the MoR with the consent and delivery evidence it holds.
