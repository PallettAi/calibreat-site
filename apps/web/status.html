Changelog

Update notes

What changed in each beta, newest first. The full build journal lives in the project’s CHANGELOG.

beta v0.0.5

Your data can leave the phone, and come back · Next build, not yet released

  • Import your diary from MyFitnessPal or Cronometer. It adds days to your diary and never replaces it. Importing the same file twice adds nothing.
  • Restore from a backup. The preview shows exactly what will change before anything is written.
  • The target stops moving when you train. A session is still recorded and shown, but it is no longer added on top of your budget.
  • Daily recap card “yesterday, calibrated”, and a weekly share card from Trends.
  • First-scan onboarding. Finishing setup lands on Home with the meal log ready.
  • Food search is UK-only. Generic foods come from the bundled CoFID 2021 dataset, and packaged products from Open Food Facts UK.
  • Licence system removed from the app and website while store payments are built.

beta v0.0.4

Logging without the friction · Released

  • Half the download. The APK now builds for the two CPU types real phones use, cutting it from 179 MB to 95 MB.
  • Fasting timer on Home, started when you finish eating.
  • True Burn trends screen with the dashboard card as the headline.
  • Recipe URL importer and photo label logging in the meal log sheet.
  • Saved meals and copy yesterday’s meal, so repeat meals take one tap.
  • Data export to a JSON backup, and a barcode-miss cache so failed lookups aren’t retried each time.
  • Performance pass on the Home streak ticker and lists.

beta v0.0.3

True Burn Learning · Released

  • True Burn Learning. Your burn is estimated from your weigh-ins and intake, and the estimate improves each week.
  • Supplement picker in setup, with a hydration hint for creatine.
  • Fixed water goal of 4,000 ml a day for everyone.
  • Editable water total, and a Settings sheet with reminder and haptics toggles.

beta v0.0.2

Instrument dashboard · Released

  • Home rebuilt as a calibration panel: a daily dial, macro gauges, a water tube and a weight horizon.
  • Calendar polished, with a Monday start and future dates dimmed.

beta v0.0.1

Locked foundation · Released

  • First build: the app scaffold, dark and light themes, the brand, and the profile and goals setup.
